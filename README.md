# Pakistan Agriculture & Livestock Analytics

A Power BI dashboard analyzing Pakistan's agricultural production, crop productivity, livestock population, livestock products, land utilization, and regional production patterns using multi-year data (2010-11 to 2025-26).

## 📊 Overview

- **Crop Categories:** 12
- **Livestock Categories:** 8
- **Regions Covered:** 5
- **Years of Data:** 16

The dashboard is built across 5 pages, moving from a high-level executive summary to a decision-support view that ties crop and livestock performance together.

## 🗂️ Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary
High-level KPIs and growth trends across livestock products and crop yields.
- Poultry meat (+268%) and eggs (+120%) show the fastest growth of any livestock product, far ahead of milk, beef and mutton (38–61%).
- Among crops, Maize (+46%), Sugarcane (+31%) and Rice (+30%) posted strong yield growth; Wheat improved only modestly (+10%).
- Cotton (-18%) and Gram (-30%) yields declined over the period — signaling weakening productivity that needs further investigation.

### 2. Crop Analysis
Production, yield, prices, and regional share for major crops.
- Punjab is the leading producer: ~76% of wheat, 82% of maize, 71% of cotton, 66% of sugarcane, and 55% of rice production.
- Yield performance is mixed: Maize, Sugarcane and Rice show strong improvement, while Wheat improved moderately and Cotton/Gram declined.
- Production ≠ productivity: high production volume does not always mean high yield. Crop decisions should consider yield, production scale, prices, and costs together.

### 3. Livestock Overview
Population trends and regional distribution of livestock.
- Dairy drives livestock output: milk dominates measured output at ~60K tonnes, far exceeding individual meat categories.
- Poultry shows stronger growth: poultry population grew faster in percentage terms, but from a much smaller base — indicating higher expansion potential rather than larger current scale.
- Livestock is regionally diversified: Punjab accounts for ~41% of livestock, while Sindh, KP and Balochistan each contribute ~19–20% — a more balanced regional landscape than crops, where Punjab accounts for ~69%.
- The livestock base is expanding: cattle, buffalo, goats and sheep show an overall upward trend, supporting continued capacity growth across the sector.

### 4. Livestock Products
Production trends for meat, milk, eggs, and byproducts.
- Dairy remains the largest growth segment: milk output increased from ~37.5M tonnes in 2010-11 to 60M+ tonnes in 2025-26, reinforcing its dominant role in livestock production.
- Poultry is reshaping the meat mix: poultry meat overtook beef and mutton combined around 2017-18 and continued to widen the gap.
- Growth varies by product: eggs and poultry meat show strong expansion, while beef and mutton grow more gradually — highlighting the need for segment-level opportunity assessment.
- Byproduct growth appears limited: hides & skins remain relatively flat despite the expanding livestock base, suggesting potential for greater value capture in downstream byproduct markets.

### 5. Decision View
Crop vs. livestock comparison to guide where to focus next.
- Wheat leads in production scale, while sugarcane achieves substantially higher yield per hectare, showing different strengths across major crops.
- Livestock shows stronger growth: poultry population increased ~211% and milk production ~61%, compared with ~17% growth in wheat production.
- Growth and scale identify opportunities, not profitability: expansion decisions should also consider prices, costs, margins, water requirements, demand and processing capacity.
